# Job / workflow name

## Purpose and ownership
Business output, technical owner, backup owner, and downstream users.

## Inputs and schedule
Source freshness requirements, warehouse/role, timezone, dependencies, and schedule.

## Execution
Exact script order, configuration, expected output grain, and normal runtime.

## Validation
Expected counts/ranges, duplicate checks, cost reconciliation, and refresh marker.

## Failure and recovery
Failure signals, partial-write handling, idempotent rerun steps, rollback, and escalation route.

## Change history
Date, change, evidence, and approval reference.
